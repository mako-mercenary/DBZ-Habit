# Card art brief: Saiyan Habits card battles

Personal-use artwork for 161 cards. One PNG per card, named exactly as listed, returned in one folder.

## Technical spec (applies to every card)

- **Size:** 768 × 960 px (4:5, portrait). PNG, sRGB.
- **No border, no text, no numbers, no logo.** The app draws the frame, rarity border, element badge, the four numbers and the name on top of the picture.
- **Safe zone:** keep the face and the important part of the pose inside the centre 60% of the width and 70% of the height. The outer edges are covered by the four numbers (top centre, bottom centre, left middle, right middle) and the name bar along the bottom 12%.
- **Board crop:** on the board the picture is cropped to almost square (centre crop), so the subject must still read when the top and bottom 10% are cut off.
- **Framing:** waist-up or three-quarter body, character fills about 75% of the height. Creatures and dragons may fill the frame.
- **Background:** simple and soft, tinted to the card's element (see the element list), so the white outlined numbers stay readable. No busy detail behind the edges.
- **Style:** consistent across all 161: bright anime cel-shading, clean dark outlines, saturated colours, soft rim light. Match the style of the board picture.
- **Lighting:** light from the upper left on every card, so the set looks uniform side by side.

## Element backgrounds

- **Ki (orange #FF8F1F):** warm orange energy glow
- **Strength (red #E53935):** deep red, rocky battleground feel
- **Spirit (green #43A047):** calm green, misty sky
- **Tech (light blue #29B6F6):** cool cyan, clean lab or city light

## Rarity borders (drawn by the app; for reference only)

The app frames each card itself. Do not paint these into the art. Listed so any preview or mock-up uses the same colours.

| Rarity | Border colour | Border style |
|---|---|---|
| Common | #B0BEC5 silver-grey | thin flat line |
| Rare | #42A5F5 blue | thin line with a soft blue glow |
| Epic | #AB47BC purple | double line, purple glow |
| Legendary | #FFC107 gold | thick gold line, sparkles in the corners |
| Signature | #FF7043 orange-red | thick line with a flame trim; challenger's own card |

On the board, the outer frame is also tinted blue for the player's cards and red for the opponent's.
