## Art

The placeholder art (silhouettes, boss shapes, the rival, the dragon, the
background, the launcher icon, the scouter earpiece, the cloud ornament and the
capsule emblem) is original, drawn by `tools/placeholder_art.py`. The UI icons
and the drawn decorations (frames, tabs, watermarks, beans, the scouter screen)
are original.

Images supplied by the owner of this app replace the placeholders; their
originals are kept in `art_sources/`.

## 3D models

Cars, city buildings and street lights in the hover-bike run are by Kay
Lousberg (https://www.kaylousberg.com), from KayKit City Builder Bits 1.0
(https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-City-Builder-Bits-1.0). Trucks,
road barriers, cones and palm trees are by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), from Car
Kit, City Kit Roads and Nature Kit. Both are released under Creative Commons
Zero (CC0, public domain). Credit is not required; it's given anyway, with
thanks. The vehicles' wheels are removed so they hover.

The 3D view is rendered with SceneView (https://github.com/SceneView/sceneview,
Apache 2.0) on Google's Filament engine (Apache 2.0).
