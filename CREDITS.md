## Art

The placeholder art (silhouettes, boss shapes, the rival, the dragon, the
background, the launcher icon, the scouter earpiece, the cloud ornament and the
capsule emblem) is original, drawn by `tools/placeholder_art.py`. The UI icons
and the drawn decorations (frames, tabs, watermarks, beans, the scouter screen)
are original.

Images supplied by the owner of this app replace the placeholders; their
originals are kept in `art_sources/`.
